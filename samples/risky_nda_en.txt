MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of
October 1, 2026 by and between Apex Dynamics Ltd. ("Disclosing Party") and the
undersigned recipient ("Receiving Party").

1. Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means any information
disclosed by either party, in any form, including without limitation business
plans, customer lists, source code, and pricing.

2. Obligations. The Receiving Party shall hold all Confidential Information in
strict confidence. The confidentiality obligations under this Agreement shall
remain in effect indefinitely and shall survive in perpetuity following the
termination of this Agreement for any reason.

3. Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue
for an initial term of two (2) years. Thereafter, this Agreement shall
automatically renew for successive one (1) year periods unless either party
provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the
expiration of the then-current term.

4. Use Restrictions. The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information
solely for the Purpose. The Receiving Party shall not engage in any business
competitive with the Disclosing Party during the term of this Agreement and for
a period of five (5) years thereafter, in any territory worldwide.

5. Intellectual Property. All inventions, works, and intellectual property
conceived by the Receiving Party during the term of this Agreement, whether or
not related to the Purpose or based on the Confidential Information, shall be
the sole and exclusive property of the Disclosing Party, including all
background intellectual property previously developed by the Receiving Party.

6. Termination. The Disclosing Party may terminate this Agreement at any time
in its sole discretion without notice or liability. Upon termination, the
Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information.

7. Liability. The Receiving Party shall be liable for any and all losses,
damages, and costs arising from any breach of this Agreement, without
limitation. The Receiving Party agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the
Disclosing Party, its affiliates, officers, and employees from any claims
arising out of this Agreement.

8. Fees. The Disclosing Party reserves the right to adjust fees or charges
associated with the Purpose at its sole discretion and without prior notice.

9. Waiver. The Receiving Party irrevocably waives any and all claims and rights
against the Disclosing Party arising from or related to this Agreement.

10. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of
Delaware, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of
Wilmington, Delaware.
